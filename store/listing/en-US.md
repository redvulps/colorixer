Colorixer: color palettes

# Short description (80)
Build harmonious palettes on a painter's color wheel or from your photos.

# Full description (4000)
Colorixer helps artists and designers build color palettes that work together.

A PAINTER'S COLOR WHEEL
The wheel follows the RYB circle used in painting: red goes with green, blue with orange, yellow with violet. Pick the base color by dragging on the wheel or with HSV or HSL sliders.

8 HARMONIES, 5 COLORS EACH
Analogous, Complementary, Split complementary, Triadic, Square, Compound, Shades and Monochromatic. Each harmony gives 5 colors ready to use, with lighter and darker versions.

PALETTES FROM YOUR PHOTOS
Pick a photo from your gallery or take one with the camera, and Colorixer takes 5 colors from it in 6 moods: Natural, Vibrant, Light, Muted, Deep and Dark. It shows where each color came from. The photo is read on your device and nothing is uploaded.

SAVE, COPY AND EXPORT
• Save the palettes you like and open them again anytime
• Copy each color as HEX, RGB, HSL or HSV
• See the palette in full screen
• Export to Procreate (.swatches) and Adobe apps (.ase)

MADE FOR PHONES AND TABLETS
On an upright tablet, Color and Palettes sit side by side; turn it and you get a three-column workspace.

In English, Portuguese and Spanish.
